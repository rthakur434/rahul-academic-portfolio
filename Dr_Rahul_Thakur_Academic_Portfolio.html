A concise overview of my academic journey and professional interests.

I am an Assistant Professor (Tech Faculty) at Vedam School of Technology, Sushant University, Gurugram. I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics at M.D. University, Rohtak, with research on the analysis of experimental designs under a fuzzy environment. I qualified CSIR-JRF in Mathematical Sciences (AIR 67, June 2024).

My academic experience includes university teaching, undergraduate instruction, and statistical subject-matter work. I emphasize conceptual understanding, step-by-step problem solving, and connecting mathematical ideas with practical applications.

My research spans fuzzy set theory and its extensions, neutrosophic statistics, information theory, fuzzy numbers, experimental design, statistical distributions, and applied decision-making. I also work with statistical software and data-analysis tools including R, Python, SPSS, Minitab, Jamovi, and Excel.

“Make complex statistical ideas understandable, useful, and meaningful.”
